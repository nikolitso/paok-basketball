The short version

What the games say about Saturday

  • Iraklis are 1–3, but better than that record. They lost at home to Olympiacos by 10, then lost twice in overtime after leading late: by 17 at Peristeri and by 12 at Doxa. Their net rating is about zero (−0.6 per 100), and schedule-adjusted they are +0.4.
  • They collapse late, and their free-throw shooting is the main reason. They make 60% of their free throws (55/91), missed six of seven at the line at the end of regulation in Lefkada, and went 9–20 in the fourth quarter at Peristeri. Lukić: "You have to play 45 minutes well; 40 isn't enough."
  • Common opponent: Doxa. PAOK beat them at home 86–73 (+18.5 per 100) without Osman and Calathes. Iraklis lost there 94–86 in overtime (−9.6 per 100).
  • The projection is PAOK 74–74, PAOK 52%. The common opponent and Iraklis's free-throw shooting suggest PAOK slightly better than that, around 55–60%. The derby crowd at the Ivanofeio and Iraklis's offensive rebounding keep it close.
  • Where Saturday will be decided: Iraklis's offensive glass (46% at Peristeri, 42% against Karditsa) against PAOK's defensive rebounding; tempo (Iraklis play fast, about 78 possessions with overtime, while PAOK grind at 69); and late-game free throws.

PAOK in the league

  • A different lineup every game. No Tyree at Mykonos; no Osman, Calathes or Motiejūnas against Doxa; no Calathes, Alexander or Tyree at Kolossos; no Mitrou-Long, Hudgins or Motiejūnas against AEK. Foreign-player limits and rest decide who plays.
  • Slow and efficient. 68–70 possessions each game, with 122–129 points per 100 in the first three games. Then 96 against AEK, including a 5-point second quarter.
  • Second halves win games. +21 in second halves across four games. Trinchieri: "We did the right things in the last quarter."
  • Who carries it: Osman (37 at Kolossos), Tyree (16 against Doxa, 28 against AEK), and Mitrou-Long and Hudgins at Mykonos. Calathes has played two of four league games.

Iraklis in the league

  • Core: Melvin (13.8 points, 7 rebounds), Diarra (12.5 points, 10 assists at Peristeri), Lecque (13.0), Styles (13.8, 24 off the bench against Karditsa) and Syllas. Coach: Zoran Lukić.
  • A good defence. They held Olympiacos to 78 and Karditsa to 53 (32.5% eFG).
  • Turnovers and decisions late. 23–25% turnover rates against Peristeri and Karditsa. Lukić after Peristeri: "In the critical moments we took wrong decisions."
  • Free throws: 7/13 against Olympiacos, 14/23 against Karditsa and 18/33 at Doxa.
  • Roster news: Vasilis Mouratos was released on 23 October. Hassan Diarra missed the FIBA Europe Cup game on 21 October through illness, then played at Doxa. Horchler and Kouk were injured early in October and have since returned.

PAOK trailed 32–37 at halftime after missing four lay-ups and six free throws early. They won the second half 56–48. Osman (18) hit the late three, and Hudgins (6/6 FT) and Mitrou-Long (14) carried the bench and guard minutes.

"When you start the game missing four lay-ups and six free throws, that's about concentration and mental toughness… No excuses: Mykonos played better. We're very bad at using our fouls… I won't use the schedule as an explanation. I don't want to give the team an alibi."Andrea Trinchieri

The week before Saturday

PAOK

Away at Bahcesehir on Tuesday (EuroCup), then the derby on Saturday. Mitrou-Long is doubtful. Trinchieri's message lately: defence first, fewer turnovers, "consistency for 40 minutes".

Iraklis

Iraklis also play midweek in the FIBA Europe Cup (2–1 so far). They lost 85–86 at home to Dziki Warszawa on 21 October after a late defensive breakdown. Lukić: "With mistakes like that you don't deserve to make the last shot." It's a derby at the Ivanofeio, and PAOK won there 76–72 in April.

What this means for the projections

  • Overtime inflates Iraklis's tempo. Their 78 possessions per game include two overtimes; regulation pace is about 72–75. The projection already expects about 71 possessions, which fits.
  • Free-throw shooting is not in the model. Iraklis's 60% from the line is a real weakness in close games, and the Greek League has had plenty of those. It argues for PAOK slightly above the 52% projection.
  • PAOK's lineup will again depend on foreign-player choices. The projection switches to the official 12 once it is announced.
  • Team-stat lines to watch: Iraklis offensive rebounds, PAOK free throws made (they average 21 in the league) and total points. Both teams' games have been low-scoring when the defence was set: 65–62 and 71–53.
