The short version

What the games say about tomorrow

  • Common opponents favour PAOK. Both beat Manresa at home, and PAOK by more (+19 vs +15 per 100 possessions). Both played away at Bosna: PAOK won 75–72, Bahcesehir lost 77–86. On the two shared opponents, PAOK is about +10 per 100 better.
  • Bahcesehir's rating is inflated by one blowout. The 100–61 at Balkan (+55 per 100) is worth about 6 points of their adjusted net rating on its own. With blowouts capped at ±25 per 100, the teams come out level: Bahcesehir +6.9, PAOK +6.4.
  • The projection is now Bahcesehir 87–82, PAOK 31% (it was 26% before the model changes below). The games themselves point to Bahcesehir by 3–5 at home, with PAOK around 35–40%.
  • Where tomorrow will be decided: Bahcesehir's offensive rebounding at home (42%) against PAOK's weak away defensive rebounding (60–64%); PAOK's fouling (opponents get 37 FTA per 100 shots); Bahcesehir's fast starts against PAOK's strong fourth quarters; and PAOK's three-point defence (opponents shoot 39%).

Manresa

Both wins were comfortable and both teams scored efficiently (57.8% eFG each). PAOK won on defence: Manresa went 5/27 from three. Bahcesehir won on volume: 42% of their own misses rebounded, 26/33 free throws and 55 bench points. Manresa came to Thessaloniki without three rotation players (Benítez, Oriola, G. Fernández) and to Istanbul without two (Bassas, Vila), so the comparison is fair.

Bosna

PAOK slowed the game to 65 possessions, forced 21.5% turnovers and won late. Bahcesehir let Bosna start 21–13 and never led after that, shooting 6/24 from three. Bosna had Popović against PAOK but not against Bahcesehir; Bahcesehir were without Isiaha Mike. Bosna coach Gjergja credited Hawkins and Vrabac for shutting down Bahcesehir's power forwards.

Two games each is a small sample. The value here is the direction: on identical opponents and venues, PAOK has been the better team, by about 10 points per 100 possessions on average.

PAOK in Europe

  • Shooting is the weak point. 29.8% from three as a team. Osman, the top scorer, is 2/18 from three in the EuroCup and scores at the rim and the line (32/44 FT).
  • Perimeter defence leaks. Lietkabelis made 13/21 threes and Ulm 9/17. Trinchieri after Lietkabelis: "We gave them 62% from the field."
  • Calathes runs the offence. With him: 14, 16 and 19 assists. Without him against Ulm: 7 assists and 21 turnovers.
  • Strong closers. +24 in fourth quarters, against −6 in the first three. Tyree (20, 14, 15, 19 points) and Foster (14 of his 21 in Lithuania came in the fourth) carry late scoring.
  • Away, the game slows (65 and 75 possessions) and PAOK are 1–1 with a −3.7 net.

Bahcesehir in Europe

  • Fast starters at home. 23–18 against Roma and 32–20 against Manresa after the first quarter; +45 over all first halves.
  • Glass and depth. 42% offensive rebounding at home, 19 second-chance points against Roma, and 55 bench points against Manresa.
  • Balanced scoring. Reed (17.3 ppg), Flynn (12.5), Cavanaugh (11.8), Avramović (11.0) and Ponitka (10.2), with Williams as the passing big (7 assists against Roma).
  • The loss came on the road against physical pressure. At Bosna: 6/24 from three, a 13-point first quarter, no offensive rebounding (25.7%), and no Isiaha Mike.
  • Form: 5–0 in the Turkish league, the latest a 100–96 home win over Beşiktaş on Saturday, decided by Flynn's late threes.

Who played

Full squad. Fillios and Kaklamanakis were dressed but did not play. Bosna had Popović (10 points) but not Banks.

What happened

Calathes had 7 assists by halftime (40–35). Bosna won the third quarter 26–19 and led by 6 at 59–53. Osman and Tyree drove PAOK back in front. Tyree's two free throws made it 75–72 with 16 seconds left, Trinchieri chose not to foul, and Bosna missed two open threes.

PAOK won on turnovers (Bosna 21.5%, 20 PAOK points off them) and offensive rebounds (37.8%). They nearly lost it on fouling: Bosna went 22/27 at the line (FT rate 52). PAOK shot 7/32 from three, and Osman went 0/7.

For projections

Away, PAOK slow the game down and grind. Their attack is efficient enough on drives and free throws, but the fouling is a recurring risk.

What this means for the projections

  • Blowouts are now capped in the ratings. Margins count up to ±25 per 100 possessions, so the Balkan game no longer dominates Bahcesehir's numbers (+13.1 → +6.9 adjusted net), while PAOK barely move (+6.4).
  • Projection after the changes: Bahcesehir 87 – PAOK 82, PAOK 31%, up from 26%. Home court now counts for a little more (2 points each way, measured on this season's games), which costs PAOK a few points of win chance away from home. The game-by-game evidence suggests slightly more, about 35–40%.
  • Calathes now counts for more than his points. A missing lead passer also lowers the team's playmaking: without him PAOK's assists fell 16 → 7 and their turnover rate rose 15 → 25%.
  • Venue effects look bigger than the 1.5-point home edge for PAOK (+14.9 home vs −3.7 away per 100), but two games each is too few to change the model. Worth watching for another month.
  • Team-stat lines to watch tomorrow: Bahcesehir offensive rebounds (15 a game at home; PAOK allowed 12 and 8 in their two away games), free throws for Bahcesehir (PAOK foul 24 times a game), PAOK threes (7.5 projected; they average 7.3 at 29.8%).
